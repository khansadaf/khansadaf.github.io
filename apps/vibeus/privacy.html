This policy applies to the VibeUs mobile app (“Application”) provided by Sadaf Khan (“Service Provider”). VibeUs is an ad-supported entertainment quiz app. This service is provided “AS IS”.

Information we collect

  • Account: an anonymous Supabase user ID created on first launch. You may type a nickname and pronoun. We do not require email or a password.
  • Quiz data: your answers, scores, and optional AI readings, stored with your anonymous ID so History works.
  • Coins: a local and server count of coins earned from ads or sharing.
  • Device and ads: on iOS we ask App Tracking Transparency permission. If you allow it, Google AdMob may use your advertising ID for relevant ads. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalized. On Android we ask in-app consent and use the Advertising ID permission required by Google Play.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not require your real name, phone number, or contacts.
  • Quizzes are for entertainment only and are not professional advice.

Third parties

  • Supabase hosts accounts, questions, results, and the AI edge function.
  • OpenAI may process a short summary of quiz answers when you spend coins on AI analysis.
  • Google AdMob serves ads and may collect device advertising identifiers as described in Google’s policies.

We may also disclose information as required by law, or to protect rights and safety.

Your choices

You can decline tracking when asked. The app still works. Uninstalling the app removes local data. Server rows tied to an anonymous ID remain until you email us to delete them.

Children

VibeUs is intended for ages 14+. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security and changes

We use HTTPS and standard safeguards. This policy may be updated. Continued use of the app means you accept the current version.
