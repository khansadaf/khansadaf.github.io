How VibeUs works

  • Pick a quiz mode (Fun, Red Flag, or Compatibility) and answer the questions.
  • See a score and reading. History saves past results on this device and, when signed in anonymously, on our server.
  • Watch a short ad to earn coins, then spend coins for an optional AI reading.
  • TrustCheck is marked coming soon and is not available yet.

Ads and tracking

On iOS, Apple may ask you to Allow or Ask App Not to Track. On Android we show an in-app choice. You can use every quiz if you decline. Ads still appear; they may be less personalized.

Delete my data

Uninstalling the app removes data stored on the device. To delete server-side quiz history tied to your anonymous account, email us from the address you use for App Store / Play contact and include “VibeUs data deletion” in the subject.
